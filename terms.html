免费功能与付费功能

普通模式可以一直免费使用。聚合模式（LeoBar 显示条）可以在首次启动后免费试用 14 天，之后需要购买年度订阅或永久版才能继续使用；试用结束后普通模式仍然可以正常使用。

免费试用

聚合模式可以在首次启动后免费使用 14 天，不需要购买，也不会自动转为付费。年度订阅另有 App Store 提供的免费试用：订阅时如显示「免费试用」，试用期结束后会按年度价格自动扣费，除非你在试用期结束前至少 24 小时取消；每个 Apple ID 只能享受一次。

订阅

年度订阅通过你的 Apple ID 付款，除非在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时关闭自动续订，否则订阅会自动续订并按相同价格扣费。你可以在 App Store 的账户设置中管理或取消订阅，取消后在已付费的周期内仍可使用。

永久版

永久版一次购买、长期有效，可通过「恢复购买」在同一 Apple ID 的其他 Mac 上恢复。

退款

所有付款由 Apple 处理，退款请通过 Apple 的报告问题页面申请。

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Contact: q80671428@gmail.com

By using LeoBar ("the app") you agree to Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA) and the terms below.

Free and paid features

Normal mode is free to use. Aggregated mode (the LeoBar bar) is free for 14 days from the first launch; after that it needs the yearly subscription or the lifetime purchase. Normal mode keeps working after the trial.

Free trial

Aggregated mode is free for 14 days from the first launch, with no purchase and nothing that turns into a payment. The yearly subscription may also come with the App Store's free trial: when the subscription shows a free trial, it is charged at the yearly price when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before; once per Apple ID.

Subscription

The yearly subscription is charged to your Apple ID and renews automatically at the same price unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings; after cancelling, it stays active until the end of the paid period.

Lifetime purchase

The lifetime purchase is paid once and does not expire. Use "Restore Purchases" to restore it on other Macs signed in with the same Apple ID.

Refunds

All payments are processed by Apple. Request refunds through Apple's Report a Problem page.

Disclaimer

The app moves other apps' menu bar icons but never changes or deletes their data. Updates to macOS or to other apps may stop some icons from being moved or recognized; we will do our best to fix this in later versions. To the extent permitted by law, the app is provided "as is".
