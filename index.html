LeoBar 支持

LeoBar 是一款 Mac 菜单栏管理工具：把不常用的菜单栏图标藏起来，需要时在 LeoBar 显示条里点开。

常见问题

图标移动不了或显示不出来？请在「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性」里确认 LeoBar 已获得「辅助功能」和「屏幕录制」权限，并在 LeoBar 的权限窗口里完成「菜单栏脚本」授权。

系统提示 LeoBar 想要控制「系统事件」？请点「允许」。LeoBar 通过它识别菜单栏图标属于哪个应用，并打开你点击的图标。

换了 Mac 或重装后怎么恢复购买？用同一个 Apple ID 登录 App Store，在 LeoBar 的购买窗口点「恢复购买」。

怎么取消订阅？在 App Store 中点你的头像 › 账户设置 › 订阅，选择 LeoBar 取消。

LeoBar Support

LeoBar is a menu bar manager for the Mac: it hides the menu bar icons you rarely use and opens them from the LeoBar bar when you need them.

FAQ

Icons don't move or don't show? In System Settings › Privacy & Security, check that LeoBar has Accessibility and Screen Recording, and authorize the menu bar script in LeoBar's permission window.

macOS asks whether LeoBar may control System Events? Click Allow. LeoBar uses it to tell which app each menu bar icon belongs to and to open the icon you click.

Restore a purchase on a new Mac or after reinstalling? Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID and click "Restore Purchases" in LeoBar's purchase window.

Cancel the subscription? In the App Store, click your name › Account Settings › Subscriptions, and cancel LeoBar.
