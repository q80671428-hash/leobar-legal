LeoBar 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日 · 联系方式：q80671428@gmail.com

LeoBar（以下简称「本应用」）是一款 Mac 菜单栏管理工具，用来隐藏和显示菜单栏图标。本应用无需注册账号，你的设置和数据只保存在你自己的 Mac 上；唯一的联网是下面「购买」一节所说的购买状态查询。

本应用使用的权限

辅助功能：用于移动其他应用的菜单栏图标（把图标移进或移出隐藏区），以及在你点击时打开它的菜单。

屏幕录制：只用于截取菜单栏图标的小图，好让被隐藏的图标在 LeoBar 的显示条里显示出来。本应用不录制、不截取菜单栏以外的任何画面。

菜单栏脚本与「系统事件」：本应用会在你授权的「应用程序脚本」文件夹里安装一个脚本，通过 macOS 的「系统事件」读取每个菜单栏图标属于哪个应用、位于哪里，并在你点击时打开它。脚本只读取菜单栏图标的位置、大小和标识，不读取其他应用的窗口内容或文字。

无账号、不追踪、无广告

本应用没有注册和登录。我们不会跨应用或网站追踪你，不展示广告，也不出售任何数据。你的菜单栏图标、设置和截图从不离开你的 Mac。

保存在你 Mac 上的数据

你的设置（如工作模式、哪些图标隐藏、快捷键）和菜单栏图标的小图只保存在你的 Mac 上。删除本应用及其数据后即被清除。

LeoBar Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Contact: q80671428@gmail.com

LeoBar ("the app") is a menu bar manager for the Mac that hides and shows menu bar icons. It works without an account and your settings stay on your Mac; its only network use is checking purchases, described under Purchases.

Permissions the app uses

Accessibility: to move other apps' menu bar icons into or out of the hidden section, and to open an icon's menu when you click it.

Screen Recording: only to capture small pictures of menu bar icons, so hidden icons can be shown in LeoBar's bar. The app does not record or capture anything outside the menu bar.

Menu bar script and System Events: the app installs a script into the Application Scripts folder you authorize, and through macOS System Events reads which app each menu bar icon belongs to and where it is, and opens it when you click it. The script reads only the position, size and identifier of menu bar icons, never other apps' windows or text.

No account, no tracking, no ads

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not track you across apps or websites, we do not show ads, and we do not sell any data. Your menu bar icons, settings and pictures never leave your Mac.

Data stored on your Mac

Your settings (such as the mode, which icons are hidden and shortcuts) and the small pictures of menu bar icons are stored only on your Mac. Deleting the app and its data removes them.

Purchases

The trial, subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active trial or purchase and to see totals of purchases. To do this, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (product, date, price, status). It does not receive your name, email, payment details or anything from your menu bar. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Diagnostics

If you share analytics with app developers in macOS System Settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. We use them only to fix bugs.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
